About Us

Pawsitive Pet Academy is Durban's home for hands-on pet care education, training future professionals and confident pet owners alike. Our focus is practical learning, real-world confidence, and a strong sense of care for every animal we work with.

Our story

Founded by Sarah Smith in 2023, Pawsitive Pet Academy began with one simple goal: to give people a practical and compassionate way to build skills in pet care and animal handling. Since then, we have helped hundreds of learners become more confident with pets, whether they are owners, aspiring professionals, or future trainers.

Our mission

We want to close the knowledge gap in pet care, nutrition, behaviour, and everyday handling by offering courses that are practical, affordable, and easy to understand. Learning should help people feel more prepared and more confident in real life.

Our vision

To be Durban's most trusted name in pet care education and the first place people think of when they want to improve their relationship with animals or prepare for a career in the pet industry.

What guides us

  • Compassion for every animal.
  • Clear practical learning.
  • Friendly, supportive teaching.
  • Affordable options for real learners.

Our goals

We want to grow our course range, expand our supporter network, and keep helping more people build skills that make a real difference in their homes, businesses, and communities.

Every learner matters to us, and every course is built to deliver confidence, clarity, and action.
