About Us

Pawsitive Pet Academy is Durban's home for hands-on pet care education, training future professionals and confident pet owners alike.

History

Founded by Sarah Smith in 2023, Pawsitive Pet Academy has helped hundreds of pet owners and future professionals build real skills in a caring, hands-on environment.

Vision

To be Durban's most trusted name in pet care education, and a launchpad for careers built on compassion and skill.

Mission

To close the knowledge gap in pet care, nutrition, and training by offering practical, affordable, and industry-recognised courses.

Goals

Expand our course offerings to reach more of the pet-owning public, grow our graduate network, and continue setting the standard for pet care education in the region.
