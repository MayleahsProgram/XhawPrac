What we offer

Our courses include dog training, grooming, animal behaviour, puppy care, first aid, and pet business management. We offer six-month programmes and shorter six-week courses.

Why Pawsitive

Founded by Sarah Smith in 2023, Pawsitive Pet Academy helps pet owners and future professionals learn the basics of animal care.

  • Practical lessons with animals
  • Courses from R750
  • Certificates after completing a course
  • Courses for owners and future professionals

How it works

Choose a course, book your place, and attend the lessons. You will learn skills that can help you care for your pet or start working with animals.
