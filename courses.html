Frequently Asked Questions

What age can my dog start training? Most puppies can start foundational learning from around 8 weeks, though the right programme depends on age, temperament, and your goals.

Do I need to bring my own pet to class? Not always. Our courses may include demo animals or guided hands-on activities, and some programmes allow learners to bring their own dog.
