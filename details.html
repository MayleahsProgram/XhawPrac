Course Details

Canine Obedience

Price: R1500

Purpose

This course helps dogs of any age build reliable obedience skills using positive, reward-based methods. It teaches owners how to communicate clearly, improve behaviour, and build trust with their pets in everyday life.

What is included

  • Basic commands and recall
  • Leash handling and control
  • Behaviour correction support
  • Positive reinforcement techniques

Who this course is for

This course is ideal for new dog owners, families who want a well-mannered dog, and anyone who wants more confidence in everyday training and communication.
